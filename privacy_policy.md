# KakaAnime Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 17, 2026

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how KakaAnime handles information when you use the application.

KakaAnime is committed to minimizing data collection and protecting user privacy.

## Information We Collect

KakaAnime does not use Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics.

The application may process information locally on your device as necessary to provide its features, such as:

- Anime and episode information
- Favorites and viewing progress
- Application settings and preferences
- Authentication information required by supported services

Information stored locally on your device is not collected by KakaAnime unless explicitly required for a feature described in this policy.

## Third-Party Services

KakaAnime may access third-party services to provide anime information, images, streaming sources, authentication, or other application functionality.

These services may have their own privacy policies and data practices. Information sent to a third-party service is handled according to that service's policies.

KakaAnime does not sell personal information.

## Crash Reports and Analytics

KakaAnime does not use Firebase Crashlytics or Firebase Analytics.

The application does not intentionally send crash reports or analytics data to Firebase.

## Authentication

If a feature requires authentication with a third-party service, authentication information is handled according to that service's policies.

Where supported, authentication credentials or tokens are stored locally on the user's device rather than being stored by KakaAnime.

## Data Storage

KakaAnime may store application data locally on the user's device, including preferences, favorites, and viewing progress.

Users can remove locally stored application data through the Android system settings or by uninstalling the application.

## Children's Privacy

KakaAnime is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when KakaAnime's features or data practices change.

The "Last Updated" date will be changed when a new version of this policy is published.

## Contact

For questions, bug reports, or privacy-related requests, please use the official KakaAnime project repository.
